The problem
02 / 12

Playing more should not
feel like project management.

The path from “I want to play” to a good game is broken in four places.

01

Discovery

Great games are buried in scattered WhatsApp chats, venue calls, and one-off social posts.

02

Compatibility

Players cannot reliably assess skill fit, game intensity, or whether the group will be a good match.

03

Coordination

Finding a time, filling a court, and settling a booking takes too much effort.

04

Trust

No-shows, last-minute exits, and missing feedback weaken the entire local sports loop.

Real-world friction, not a lack of player intent.

Built with Google Cloud
10 / 12

Google AI that helps,
without deciding.

CourtMate uses Gemini for understanding and discovery, while verified data and deterministic rules remain authoritative.

Gemini parses natural-language requests, produces grounded discovery explanations, and reads optional wearable screenshots.

Firestore remains the source of truth for availability, privacy, join decisions, attendance, and CMR.

Deterministic fallbacks keep the core search and matching experience available when AI is unavailable.
